Before you enquire

The questions that usually come first.

The first year of .com registration is included in the $80 website. After that, renewal is the registrar’s price, about $14 a year, with no agency markup.

Meta includes 1,000 free customer-service conversations a month. Marketing broadcasts, and anything past that allowance, are billed by Meta to your own business account. We set the account up so you pay Meta directly, at their price.

The source, the domain, the hosting, and the WhatsApp accounts are yours. There is a 30-day warranty for faults, plus a walkthrough your team can keep. The $35 care plan is optional, for updates, backups, and monitoring.

Third-party model fees past the testing credit, licensed stock photography, ongoing blog writing, and a payment processor’s own onboarding charges sit outside the base fee. We say so before the work starts.

A standard website or a lead-capture bot is typically live in three to five business days once the content and the brief are approved.
